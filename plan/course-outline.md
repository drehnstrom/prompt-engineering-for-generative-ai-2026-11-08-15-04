# Course Title: Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (1 hour)

## Course Description

Generative AI models often produce vague, inaccurate, or unstructured outputs when given underspecified prompts. Without systematic prompt construction, users waste significant time refining queries through trial and error.

This course teaches fundamental prompt engineering techniques to help learners write clear, structured, and reliable prompts. Students practice establishing personas, defining explicit constraints, providing few-shot examples, and iterating on prompts using Google Gemini.

## Learning Objectives

- Construct effective prompts to generate high quality results from generative AI models
- Apply core prompt engineering techniques, personas, and structured formats in Google Gemini

## Who Should Attend

Beginners and professionals who want to communicate effectively with generative AI models to achieve reliable, high quality outputs.

## Prerequisites

- Basic familiarity with generative AI concepts

## Hands-On Activities

- Lab: Prompt Engineering with Google Gemini

**Module 1: Prompt Engineering Fundamentals**
